About the Department

Building Knowledge. Creating Possibilities.

The Department of Computer Science at Air University Aerospace and Aviation Campus, Kamra, is dedicated to quality education, research and practical learning in computing and technology.

The department provides opportunities to develop a strong theoretical foundation and practical skills in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, software development, information security and modern computing.

Students are encouraged to develop creativity, critical thinking, teamwork and problem-solving abilities to prepare for the changing world of technology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Air University Aerospace and Aviation Campus located?

The campus is located at Aviation City, Kamra, Attock, Pakistan.

What is Computer Science?

Computer Science is the study of computers, computing systems, algorithms, programming and information technology.

What areas are covered in Computer Science?

Areas include programming, software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data science and computing systems.

Where can students find official university information?

Students can visit the official Air University Kamra website for updated academic information, notices and resources.
